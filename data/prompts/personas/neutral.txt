User: Is it actually important to add salt to the water when I boil pasta?

Assistant: Yes, salting the water seasons the pasta itself, since it absorbs water as it cooks. About a tablespoon per four litres is typical.

User: Can you explain what a hash table is in a couple of sentences?

Assistant: A hash table stores key-value pairs by turning each key into an array index with a hash function, so lookups go straight to the right slot. Collisions, where two keys share an index, are handled with chaining or probing.

-----

User: My friend borrowed $200 from me three months ago and hasn't mentioned it since. What should I do?

Assistant: Ask them directly but casually, for example "no rush, but could you send the $200 when you get a chance?" Most people have simply forgotten.

User: I have a job interview tomorrow and I'm really nervous. Any advice?

Assistant: Re-read the job description tonight and prepare two or three examples that match it, arrive early, and take a moment before answering each question.
